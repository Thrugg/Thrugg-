Moving abroad: where the gains land

A move abroad is the largest tax lever an investor has. This module prices the embedded gain at the border, then compounds the same portfolio for N years under each destination's rules, wealth tax included.

Options lab

Prices every leg with Black-Scholes-Merton (European) or a Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial tree (American, early exercise). It projects the position across price and time, like optionsprofitcalculator.com, and adds probability, Greeks and the after-tax result in your country.

IBKR statement import

Paste or drop an Interactive Brokers Flex Query or Activity Statement CSV. Trades are bucketed into the baskets your tax residence uses, converted to euros, and can be pushed into the Tax desk. Nothing leaves your browser.
